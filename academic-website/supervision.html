Dissertation Enquiries

I supervise BSc, MComp, and MSc projects in reinforcement learning, particularly hierarchical reinforcement learning and applications to compelling real-world problems. If these areas interest you, please get in touch once dissertation supervisor selection begins; I would be happy to discuss your ideas.

I would love to supervise all the brilliant students interested in these topics, but please bear in mind that my workload varies each year and I may not have capacity for everyone.
